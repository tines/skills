# Status updates

Gather the relevant work for the requested period before drafting. Organize the update around outcomes rather than activity:

- What changed or shipped
- Why it matters
- What is in progress
- Risks or blockers
- Decisions or help needed
- What happens next

Use concrete names, links, and measurements where available. Do not invent progress from assignment alone. Match the audience and requested format, and keep routine updates concise.
